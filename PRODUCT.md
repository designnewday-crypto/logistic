# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business shippers evaluating road and sea freight and preparing a shipment enquiry.

## Product Purpose

English-language website for a logistics company with an office in Burgas, Bulgaria.
Help visitors understand services, explore routes and prepare a useful enquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve twelve homepage sections, partner strip, footer and working navigation,
region tabs, cargo selection, guides, FAQ, review carousel and two independent forms.
Forms validate, review and download an enquiry locally; delivery is not connected.
The build is a self-contained HTML page. Keep the existing Vite/vanilla JS stack.

## Brand Commitments

The user selected design/prototype-3.jpg and rejected radical style replacement.
Retain its recognisable dark-blue foundation, bold typography and port photography.
Make the overall website visibly less like Cargov through composition and hierarchy.
The company name Burgas Logistics remains provisional.

## Evidence on Hand

Office location Burgas, Bulgaria is user-confirmed. Company figures, routes, partner
names and testimonials are explicitly user-authorized fictional demonstration data,
not verified proof; retain clear labels. Photos are generated concept imagery,
not company documentary evidence. Existing data resides in src/content.js.

## Product Principles

- Preserve useful content while giving the site its own visual character.
- Make shipment enquiry the main action.
- Show the entire website when judging the design.
- Keep demo claims and form-delivery limitations clear.

## Delivery

GitHub previews and ZIP downloads work for this user. Chat file-artifact links have
failed repeatedly. Use the website-prototype branch for reviewable updates.
