# Homepage direction contract

Mode: Persuade. Existing site, existing selected visual authority; a code-led revision
of the incumbent full page, not a new branding workshop. User requested execution
with Taste Skill and Impeccable after rejecting radical visual-world replacement.
No persistent build-path preference is inferred.

## World

Burgas as a departure point. Preserve dark blue, lime, bold Inter typography,
port photographs, English language and all twelve sections. Borrow the reading
clarity of a port departure board: origin, destination, mode, next action.
Taste direction: borrowed-category, seed 1239818412, palette/type kept.
The dealt counter-object hero needs a consumer product that this company does not
have; the user-pinned photo/enquiry hero takes priority. No dice re-roll.

## First viewport

A large port photograph anchors the left side of a flat navy hero; readable
white/lime heading and a white enquiry form. No orbital artwork or giant containing
rounded card. Mobile presents the statement, a useful quote link, port image and form.

## Visitor path

Understand the Burgas base, compare four services, choose a sample corridor,
select cargo, learn the three practical steps, read client examples and guides,
resolve questions, and prepare an enquiry. All claims remain demo-labelled.

## Signature interaction

A route selection prefills the enquiry and makes collection/delivery the next step.
This works by keyboard and touch. Motion supports reading: small staggered entries,
slow port-image drift, and control feedback, disabled with reduced motion.

## Full-page reach

Photo-and-list services; unboxed route board; photographic cargo catalogue;
ordered process prose; typographic benefits; quiet partner names; one large review;
readable guide list; native FAQ; office panorama; lime enquiry close; large navy footer.

## Honest limits

Name, service details, statistics, corridors, partners and reviews remain concept
content. Generated photographs are not documentary company evidence. Forms only
review/download; no delivery backend or live tracking. Preserve these disclosures.
